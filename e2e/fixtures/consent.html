Slow-roast tomatoes

I first made these in a rented kitchen in Sicily with a landlady who did not believe in measuring anything, and I have been chasing that afternoon ever since. She would laugh at the idea of a recipe.

You need 1 kg of ripe plum tomatoes, 4 tablespoons of olive oil, 2 cloves of garlic, a teaspoon of sugar and a teaspoon of flaky salt. Heat the oven to 140 °C.

Halve the tomatoes and lay them cut side up on a tray; do not crowd them, or they will steam instead of roasting. Slice the garlic thinly and tuck a slice under each half.

Roast for 2 hours and 30 minutes, until the edges have darkened and the centres have collapsed. They keep in the fridge under oil for a week.
