osso

Just the bone. Every page arrives with its filler faded and its substance left in the author's own ink.

Key

When Osso reads a page, it sends the page's title, its language and the words the page displays, with any words you ask it to keep or highlight, to api.typesafe.ai only, over your key, to judge which sentences are filler. Never the page's address, and never what you type into a page. Which pages it reads is set under Read pages below. Osso does not read a page showing a password or card field, and holds back on pages that look private; those checks catch the usual shapes, not every one. Your key is kept in this browser, where only Osso's background worker uses it (pages never see it), and is sent only to api.typesafe.ai, with each request. Past answers are kept under a fingerprint of the text, without the text or the address.
