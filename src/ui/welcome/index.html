osso

Just the bone.

Most of what you read online is filler. Osso strikes it out, on every page, and leaves what you came for. Two choices, and you are done.

  1. Your TypeSafe key

    Osso asks a model, Jev, about every sentence of a page. It runs on a key of your own: no account with us, no server of ours. Get a key at typesafe.ai

    When Osso reads a page, it sends the page's title, its language and the words the page displays, with any words you ask it to keep or highlight, to api.typesafe.ai only, over your key, to judge which sentences are filler. Never the page's address, and never what you type into a page. It reads every page you open, as it loads, unless you choose Only when I click below. Your key is kept in this browser and sent only to api.typesafe.ai, with each request.

  2. When should Osso read a page?

    • Osso never sends the page's address, or anything you type into it.
    • It never reads a page that shows a password, card or one-time-code field.
    • It asks before reading a page that looks private, like an account area or an intranet.
    • It stays off well-known mail, document, chat, code, search, video and social sites. Edit the list

    These checks go by how a page is built, never by what it is about. They catch the usual shapes, not every one, and a site not on the list is read like any other. Where the text itself is private, choose Only when I click, or switch Osso off for that site.
